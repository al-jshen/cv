\section*{Research Interests}
\hrulesp{2mm}
I research deep neural networks that learn efficiently and generalize robustly through self-supervision and principled, domain-informed architectural biases.
I am particularly interested in multimodal foundation models and generative approaches that combine disparate data and transfer across domains.
