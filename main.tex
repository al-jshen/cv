\section*{Research Experience}
\hrulesp{-2mm}

\begin{xltabular}{\textwidth}{lX}
  2021 May - 2021 Aug & \textbf{Undergraduate Summer Research Fellowship} \\
                      & \textit{Canadian Institute for Theoretical Astrophysics} \\
                      & Continued previous work on the mass distribution of the Milky Way. Collaborated with members of the \href{http://h3survey.rc.fas.harvard.edu/}{H3 Survey} and published a first-author paper in \textit{ApJ}. \\
                      & Began a project with \href{https://joshspeagle.github.io/}{Josh Speagle} using normalizing flows to make probabilistic predictions of the ages of stars. Began a project with Norm Murray using Stan to fit dynamical models to geological data from thermal tides. \\
  2020 Aug - 2021 May & \textbf{Undergraduate Research Course} \\
                      & \textit{Canadian Institute for Theoretical Astrophysics} \\
                      & Supervised by \href{https://www.astro.utoronto.ca/~eadie/intro.html}{Prof. Gwendolyn Eadie} and \href{https://www.cita.utoronto.ca/~murray/}{Prof. Norman Murray}. Study of mass distribution of the Milky Way in a Bayesian framework. Implemented a hierarchical Bayesian model for modelling the mass of galactic halos using a gradient-based sampling algorithm, increasing computational efficiency by two orders of magnitude over existing code. \\
  2020 May - 2020 Aug & \textbf{Summer Undergraduate Research Program} \\
                      & \textit{Dunlap Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics, University of Toronto} \\
                      & Supervised by \href{https://phas.ubc.ca/users/allison-man}{Prof. Allison Man}. Study of molecular gas in a strongly-lensed galaxy group at $z=2.9$. Analyzed ALMA data to infer molecular gas masses in several high-redshift galaxies. Resulted in a first-author publication.
\end{xltabular}
